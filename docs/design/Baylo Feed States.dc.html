2eTop bar limits
Each strip is 390 px wide, then the same bar at 360 px. Leaves uses tabular numerals so 1,240 and 9,999 occupy identical width; the wordmark holds and the two icons stay 44 px. At 360 px the bell and message buttons tighten to a 40 × 44 px hit area, keeping the 44 px minimum height.

Build notes
Leaves pill: fixed height 34, tabular numerals, never shrinks — the wordmark yields first. Above 99,999 Leaves, format as 9.9k.
Badge: caps at 99+, 17 px tall, 2 px surface-coloured ring so it reads over any icon.
Title: 2-line clamp at 17/1.3 — about 62 characters before ellipsis.
Photo: aspect ratio clamped between 16/9 and 4/5, centre-cropped, so scroll rhythm survives mixed uploads.
